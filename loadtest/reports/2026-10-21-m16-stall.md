# M16 the 300-second stall, 2026-10-21

**Verdict:** The stall is not in the product. It is a 10 s DNS lookup in the load generator: the resolver of this WSL 2 machine never answers the IPv4 (`A`) query for `turnbase.localhost`, and k6 repeats the lookup every 5 minutes (its default `dns.ttl`), so every VU waits about 10 s on each expiry (`ws_connecting` max 10.0 s, a pile-up of 126 to 143 VUs, a `room_join` tail). With the harness resolving once (`dns.ttl = inf`) a 15-minute `start-rate` at 16 games/s has `ws_connecting` max 13 ms, `room_join` p99 30.7 ms, 0 errors, 0 dropped iterations. No chart or platform change was needed, and none was made. Single-node, laptop result.

## 2. What a stall looks like

From run 1's `k6_*` series (1 s resolution) and logs, UTC; scenario start 22:45:56.

- k6 pushes nothing to Prometheus for 13 to 14 s (+293 to +306 and +599 to +613) and its CPU in `samples.csv` is 1% (at +298 and +603), then 61 and 67% for the catch-up. The VU count goes 64 → 144 → 2 and 63 → 143 → 2 in run 1 (64 → 126 → 2 and 63 → 143 → 2 in run 2): the arrival-rate executor keeps starting iterations that all wait.
- `ws_connecting` max and `room_join` max jump to 10.0 s and 0.73 s (+306), and 10.0 s and 1.02 s (+613), when the waiting requests finally go.
- The ingress controller received **no request** between 22:50:47 and 22:50:56 and between 22:55:54 and 22:56:04 (its access log has 96 lines per second before, none during, 314 to 671 per second right after). So the traffic never left the generator.

## 4. The start of a cold run

Run 1 started right after `reset.sh`. Its first 29 s are a different, known effect: 160 dropped iterations (all before +29 s), 100 `move_timeout` (all at +26 s), `ws_connecting` max 2.84 s at +29 s, a 2.3 to 3 s `DELETE …/participants/me` in the ingress log at 22:46:22, and the platform logging `Failed to export spans` to Tempo and dropping 712 and 1,749 spans. It is the cold JIT of a freshly started platform and Game Host under 16 new games per second at once (`IMP-M16-03` and `04` measured the cold boot). It is not periodic and it is outside the item. Runs 2 to 4 were warm and show none of it. Run 4 started on a stack that had already played two runs; its `ws_connecting` max is 13 ms.

## 5. The cause

`getent ahostsv4 turnbase.localhost` takes about 10 s and fails; `dig +tries=1 +time=15 turnbase.localhost A` times out at the WSL resolver `10.255.255.254`; `dig … AAAA` answers `::1` in 36 ms; `curl` resolves the `.localhost` name itself and never asks. So a lookup that includes the `A` record costs about 10 s (the Go resolver in k6 waits for both answers).

k6 caches a resolved name for `dns.ttl` (default 5 m) and resolves again on the first **dial** after it expires. The harness opens two WebSockets per iteration, so a dial happens within milliseconds of any expiry, and all VUs that dial in the next 10 s wait on the same lookup. The first lookup is also slow, but it happens in `setup()`, outside the measured window. The period is 5 min plus the 10 s the lookup takes: 306 s, as measured (stalls at +293/+306 and +599/+613).

A minimal k6 script (`GET /api/games`, one VU, `noConnectionReuse`) shows it directly. The `blocked` timing of the slow request is the lookup:

| `dns.ttl` | Slow requests (`blocked`) in 100 s |
|---|---|
| `20s` | 4: 9.80 s at 23:40:51, 9.81 s at 23:41:20, 9.81 s at 23:41:50, 9.82 s at 23:42:20 (every 20 s ttl + 10 s) |
| `inf` | 1: 10.0 s at the start only |

(With keep-alive on, the single connection is reused and no lookup happens after the first, which is why the first attempt with `ttl=20s` showed one slow request only.)

The fix is in the harness: `loadtest/lib/config.js` exports `dnsOption = { ttl: 'inf' }`, and `lib/shape.js` `baseOptions`, `scenarios/smoke.js` and `scenarios/provision.js` set `dns: dnsOption`. Run 4 is the 15-minute rerun with it.

## 6. Conclusion and what it changes

- Finding 10 (the recurring 10 s connect stall) is a **harness artefact of this machine's DNS**, not a platform, ingress, Postgres or Redis stall. The platform was idle during it, as Phase A already noted.
- Finding 3 (`room_join` tail stops the `ramp-to-break` at 80 pairs) shared the same tail in `start-rate` (the p99 of 394 to 622 ms with a max of 1.0 to 1.5 s is the stall's backlog). Whether the `ramp-to-break` ceiling moves is a question for `IMP-M16-14`; `ramp-to-break` and `parallelism` use `baseOptions` and now resolve once too.
- Earlier Phase A numbers that contain a stall (the `start-rate` 32 games/s step with 146 dropped iterations, the `contexts` run's 10 s connect, the ceiling of `ramp-to-break`) are affected by it; `IMP-M16-14` reruns them with the fix.
- No Phase B note about a periodic stall is needed. For a Phase B generator that resolves a `.localhost` name through this resolver: use `dns.ttl = inf`, or `HOST_IP`.
